## PDF caveats to retain on the website

- ICT is a framework, not a guaranteed edge.
- The playbook says independent audited statistics for several named ICT models were not located.
- Spot gold or spot Bitcoin tests must not be represented as futures performance proof.
- Popularity or practitioner usage is not equivalent to profitability.
- Tick values, margins, hours and expiry must be checked against current official exchange and broker sources.
- International futures access, product permission and jurisdictional rules require separate user-specific verification; do not turn the PDF's general note into legal advice.
- YouTube/Reddit/Discord coverage in the PDF is explicitly incomplete.
- ICT macros are omitted in the PDF because exact lists vary among sources; do not claim a canonical complete list without stronger evidence.

## Content migration audit fields

For each PDF item store:
- PDF page number and heading.
- Canonical concept/instrument/event ID.
- Destination route.
- Content owner.
- Source evidence label.
- Data/spec verification state.
- Video/source URL status.
- Last review date.
- Acceptance test reference.
- Deferred or intentionally excluded reason.

A PDF topic is considered migrated only when a working route/page exists, the content is source-labelled, required fields are present, links are checked and its traceability row points to the final route/test. Copying text to a page is not sufficient.
