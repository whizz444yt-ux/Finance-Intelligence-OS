Data and research boundaries

Public macro and CFTC reports have provider-specific publication cadence. COT positioning is weekly, not intraday exchange volume. CME/ICE real-time quotes, full-depth order books and redistribution rights require separately verified market-data access. No broker order execution is connected, and unverified contract reference values are not trading instructions.
