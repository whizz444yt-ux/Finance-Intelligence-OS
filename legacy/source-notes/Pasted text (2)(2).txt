# Category 2 — India Not Available: Blocked or Not Cleared for Real-Money Use

This category needs a distinction between explicitly blocked by the Indian government, restricted by a platform's own rules, and not legally cleared for real-money participation from India. Those are not always the same status.

Polymarket — India access blocked
