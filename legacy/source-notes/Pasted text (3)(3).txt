### Is data ko kaise interpret karein?

Kisi fund ne ek company ke shares own kiye, iska matlab ye nahi ki fund ne aaj hi buy kiya hai. Isi tarah, executive ki transaction sale, purchase, option exercise ya kisi aur transaction type ki ho sakti hai.

“Insider news” ke liye safe aur useful approach: publicly disclosed insider transactions, beneficial ownership filings, institutional reports aur company announcements track karo. Material non-public information ko paid Telegram groups ya unverified “leaks” se khareedna reliable research method nahi hai.

### Central-bank research mein kya dekhna hai?

- Fed: rates, inflation outlook aur policy guidance.
- ECB / BoE / BoJ: local currency, yields aur global liquidity ka context.
- BLS / BEA: actual economic release ko previous reading aur consensus ke against compare karna.
- BIS / IMF: ek country ki news ke badle larger global credit aur financial conditions ko samajhna.

Market mein sirf actual number matter nahi karta. Actual, consensus aur previous value ke beech difference, revisions aur market ki pehle se bani expectations bhi important hoti hain.

### API choose karte waqt yeh 6 cheezein verify karna

- Coverage: Global exchanges hain ya sirf US?
- Latency: Real-time, delayed, end-of-day ya periodically updated?
- History: Intraday history kitni available hai?
- Limits: Requests per minute/day aur subscription restrictions kya hain?
- Licensing: Personal research, internal business use aur public redistribution ke rights alag ho sakte hain.
- Reliability: Missing data, symbol changes, splits, revisions aur timestamps ko kaise handle karte hain?

Ek provider ko poore global finance universe ka complete source samajhna galat hoga. Aksar price data, filings, news aur macro data ke liye alag providers ki zaroorat hoti hai.

# 14. Roz ka Global Market Research Workflow

Aap daily research ke liye is order ko use kar sakte ho:

1. Economic calendar: Fed, inflation, employment, GDP aur central-bank events check karo.
2. News and filings: Company announcements, earnings results, regulatory filings aur major geopolitical developments padho.
3. Macro context: US dollar, Treasury yields aur relevant country ke rates compare karo.
4. Market positioning: Relevant futures ke COT reports, reported short interest, ETF flows aur derivatives metrics dekho.
5. Actual price reaction: Gold, Forex, equity indices, oil ya crypto mein price structure, liquidity aur volatility verify karo.
6. Decision log: Bullish, bearish aur neutral scenarios likho; entry se pehle invalidation level, stop-loss aur risk/reward define karo.

COT aur many institutional disclosures delayed hote hain, isliye unhe background context ke taur par use karo. Intraday trade ke liye current price, available order-book/market data aur real-time event information ki freshness alag se verify karni hogi.

## Final recommendation

Aapke liye sabse valuable combination hoga official filings + macroeconomic data + current market data + positioning + AI-assisted research. Sirf 100 websites add kar dena intelligence nahi banata; actual value tab aati hai jab data comparable, timestamped aur original sources se verifiable ho.

Agar aap is directory ko coding project mein convert kar rahe ho, to pehle SEC + FRED + official economic calendars + price-data provider + CFTC + EIA + crypto analytics APIs se ek working prototype banao. Uske baad paid news feeds, institutional terminals aur advanced alternative data ko actual need ke according add karo.

Aur ek essential rule: kisi bhi website ka signal automatic trade instruction nahi hona chahiye. Original disclosure verify karo, data freshness check karo aur trade ko independent price action aur defined risk ke against evaluate karo.
