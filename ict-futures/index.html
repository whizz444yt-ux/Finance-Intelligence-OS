Daily bias checklist

  1. Which unclaimed liquidity pool is the higher-timeframe draw?
  2. Is price in premium or discount of a defined dealing range?
  3. Where are midnight open and 09:30 ET open?
  4. Which Asian/London high or low remains untouched?
  5. What red-folder news is scheduled, and when?

A killzone tells you when to pay attention; liquidity tells you where; the sweep and displacement/MSS form the trigger. Do not derive daily bias from the 1-minute chart.

Event execution protocol

Before · T−30 minutes
Mark PDH/PDL and session extremes; decide whether to trade or stay flat; calculate stops; consider reducing open risk.
Prepare
During · 0–2 minutes
Expect spreads and slippage to widen. The playbook recommends no entry into the immediate release impulse.
Stand aside
After · 2–15 minutes
Wait for a fresh sweep → MSS/displacement → pullback. The first move is not automatically the setup.

Methodology & risk disclosure

How this page separates contract facts, ICT conventions and evidence quality.

Contract specifications

Reference values are transcribed from the supplied playbook and/or linked exchange pages. Specs, active month, margin, trading hours and tick-value conventions can change; verify before order entry.

ICT models

The playbook reports no audited public statistics for Silver Bullet, Model 2022 and SMT. Vendor/spot-market tests are not equivalent to independent CME futures backtests.

News and social sources

A headline, social post, COT report, analyst opinion or positioning change is not a trading signal. Preserve source, author, timestamp, contract month and invalidation.

Educational information only; not investment advice, a recommendation, a broker or an order-routing service. Futures are leveraged and can result in losses greater than expected. This site does not provide guaranteed accuracy, a verified win rate or real-time market data by itself.
