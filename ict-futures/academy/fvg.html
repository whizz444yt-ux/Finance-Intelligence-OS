Fair Value Gap (FVG)

Three-candle price imbalance. Bullish: candle 3 low is above candle 1 high. Bearish: candle 3 high is below candle 1 low. It is an entry zone in some models, not a signal by itself.

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Identify first and third candles.
  2. For bullish, mark candle 1 high to candle 3 low; reverse for bearish.
  3. Mark 50% CE if used.
  4. Define wick-touch versus body-close mitigation/invalidation.
Require liquidity, displacement, structure and an active window as context.

Common mistakes and limitations

The PDF reports no robust proof that FVGs outperform equal-sized random zones; fills are not guaranteed.
