FVG Continuation Model

A continuation idea in which an impulsive move aligned with higher-timeframe context leaves an FVG and price later retraces into the imbalance before potentially continuing.

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Set higher-timeframe context and the next liquidity objective.
  2. Identify a clean displacement leg in the same direction.
  3. Mark the FVG and define wick/close mitigation in advance.
  4. Wait for the predeclared pullback/confirmation; put invalidation beyond the chosen zone or structure.
Use only when the gap forms during a well-defined impulsive leg and broader context remains valid.

Common mistakes and limitations

Assuming every FVG fills; entering against higher-timeframe location; confusing reversal and continuation conditions.

Evidence and source note

SUPPLEMENTAL · secondary terminology; definitions may vary · no audited strategy edge implied

A structured supplemental model assembled from common FVG usage. The supplied playbook says an FVG is an entry zone, not a signal, and does not prove that every gap fills.
