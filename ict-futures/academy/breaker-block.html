Breaker Block

A failed Order Block whose polarity is treated as reversed after price closes through it. A return may be studied as a pullback.

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Identify original OB.
  2. Require qualifying close through zone.
  3. Re-label role only after invalidation.
  4. Wait for return and separate trigger if required.
Study after liquidity raid and shift.

Common mistakes and limitations

A wick through the block alone may not meet the PDF's body-close description.
