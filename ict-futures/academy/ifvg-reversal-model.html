Inversion FVG (IFVG) Reversal Model

The supplied playbook defines an IFVG when price body-closes through the far boundary of an existing FVG, changing its role. A retest entry is a further model convention.

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Mark the original bullish or bearish FVG and record its direction.
  2. Wait for a body close through the far boundary; a wick alone does not qualify under the playbook rule.
  3. Mark the inverse support/resistance role and wait for a retest if that is the written plan.
  4. Set invalidation beyond the inverted zone and target the next opposing liquidity.
Use as an advanced pattern after understanding original FVG boundaries and close-based invalidation.

Common mistakes and limitations

Calling any wick-through an inversion; confusing a session gap with a three-candle FVG; entering without an invalidation rule.
