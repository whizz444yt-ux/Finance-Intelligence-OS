Equal Highs (EQH)

Two or more swing highs at approximately similar prices. The area above them is considered potential BSL.

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Choose a tolerance in ticks or percentage terms.
  2. Mark highs after pivots are confirmed.
  3. Use the cluster as a reference area.
Identify potential upside liquidity pools.

Common mistakes and limitations

Do not redraw tolerance after seeing the outcome.
