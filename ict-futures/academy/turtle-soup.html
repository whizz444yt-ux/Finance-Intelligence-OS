Turtle Soup (false-breakout setup)

A false breakout of a prior high or low that fails and reverses back through the level. The label originated outside ICT and is also used in later SMC/ICT discussions.

Open interactive ICT Academy →Official ICT channel ↗Official playlists ↗

How to identify and use this concept

  1. Mark a clearly defined prior high or low before price approaches it.
  2. Observe a trade beyond the level and whether price is accepted outside or returns back through it.
  3. Require a separate reversal trigger such as displacement, CISD or MSS if this is part of the written rules.
  4. Define the stop beyond the failed-break extreme and target the next opposing liquidity.
Study as a false-breakout/liquidity-trap pattern; do not attribute its invention to ICT.

Common mistakes and limitations

Calling every breakout wick Turtle Soup; ignoring trend, event risk or session context; entering without a defined reversal trigger.
